01 · Services

From risk to reduction.

One cycle: assess the risk, implement the controls that reduce it, and run the program that keeps it down. Privacy runs on the same program, and threat intelligence keeps the risk picture current.

  1. 01

    Cyber risk assessment

    We identify the scenarios that could actually hurt your organization, estimate how likely they are from threat data and your own incident history, and weigh them against the controls you have. You get a risk register leadership can act on and a treatment plan ranked by the risk each action removes.

    Risk register · Threat-informed likelihood · Ranked treatment plan

  2. 02

    Control implementation

    A gap assessment against NIST CSF 2.0 or ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A, then the controls themselves: designed, rolled out and documented with policies and evidence that hold up in an audit. Each control is there for the risk it reduces.

    Gap assessment · Control rollout · Policies and audit evidence

  3. 03

    Security and privacy program management

    We run the program with you over time: governance and ownership, risk register upkeep, metrics and board reporting, internal audit and management review. When certification is the goal, we prepare you for ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27701; the certificate itself is issued by an accredited body.

    Governance · Board reporting · Internal audit · Certification readiness

  4. 04

    Data protection

    DPO as a service, data protection impact assessments and records of processing under Brazil's LGPD and the EU's GDPR, structured on ISO/IEC 27701 and the NIST Privacy Framework. When we are your DPO, we do not also run your security program: the DPO has to be able to monitor it independently.

    DPO as a service · DPIA · Records of processing · LGPD · GDPR

  5. 05

    Threat intelligence for risk decisions

    The evidence behind the likelihood estimates: sector threat landscapes, profiles of the actors relevant to you, exposure monitoring and advisories. When the threats change, your risk register changes with them.

    Sector landscapes · Actor profiles · Exposure monitoring · Advisories

02 · Frameworks

Built on the frameworks auditors and boards already know.

One control set, mapped across all four, with LGPD and GDPR obligations on top. A single piece of evidence serves several audits.

NIST CSF 2.0

Cybersecurity Framework

The shared map of outcomes across Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. We use it to set your current and target profiles and to report progress to leadership.

ISO/IEC 27001

Information security management system

The management system that keeps the program running: risk assessment and treatment, the Statement of Applicability, Annex A controls, internal audit and management review.

ISO/IEC 27701

Privacy information management system

Brings personal data into the same management system, with controls for controllers and processors. It gives LGPD and GDPR compliance a structure that can be audited and certified.

NIST Privacy Framework

Privacy risk management

Privacy outcomes that sit alongside the CSF and cover risks that come from processing data, not only from breaches. We use it to turn legal obligations into measurable outcomes.

03 · Method

Every control traces back to a risk.

Risk first
Controls are chosen and ordered by the risk they reduce, not by a checklist.
Evidence-based
Likelihood comes from threat data and your own history. Every estimate states its source and confidence.
Framework-aligned
Work maps to NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701 and the NIST Privacy Framework, so it holds up in an audit.
Your decision
You own and accept risk. We make the trade-offs explicit and put every risk decision in writing.
